# SOAP and WSDL lab

Scenario: delivery system is old and accepts only SOAP requests.

Student tasks:

1. Open `delivery.wsdl`.
2. Find service name.
3. Find operation `CreateDelivery`.
4. Find required fields: `requestId`, `orderId`, `city`, `address`.
5. Explain how WSDL differs from OpenAPI.

Questions:

1. Where are available operations described?
2. Where are request fields described?
3. Why can an analyst meet SOAP in enterprise systems?
