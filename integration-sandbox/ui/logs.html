Logs

Case: find the integration chain by request_id

The student searches logs and restores what happened across several systems.

Business situation

Customer says order was created, but delivery did not start. The analyst searches all services by one correlation identifier.

Log examples

{"service":"order-api","request_id":"req-2026-0001","message":"POST /orders accepted","order_id":"ord-10001"}
{"service":"orders-db","request_id":"req-2026-0001","message":"order row inserted","order_id":"ord-10001"}
{"service":"events","request_id":"req-2026-0001","message":"order_created published","topic":"orders.events.v1"}
{"service":"delivery-soap","request_id":"req-2026-0001","level":"ERROR","message":"address validation failed"}

Student tasks

  1. Find all log lines by request_id.
  2. Restore the chain step by step.
  3. Find failed service.
  4. Decide which team should investigate.

Analyst takeaway

Good logs are part of requirements. Analyst should ask for request_id, business identifiers, service name, error type and clear message.
